# StreamySpace Beta: Tester Guide

## Welcome

StreamySpace is a Windows app that puts your streams and chats side by side in one window, and you are helping me find out whether it is good, easy to use and worth paying for.

What it does:

- Shows up to 4 livestreams and 4 chats at once from Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook Live and Kick.
- **Unified Chat** combines several chats into one feed, so you can read and reply in one place.
- **Auto-Monitor** outlines messages that may need a closer look. It only highlights: it never bans, times out or deletes anything by itself.
- With your own accounts connected, you can delete messages, time out, ban or unban users from the feed.

What I am asking of you: use it in your real moderating whenever it suits you between now and 16 January 2027, then fill in the short final feedback form (about 10 minutes). Honest feedback is the most useful kind, including "I would not use this" and why.

## Before you start

You need a Windows 10 or 11 PC. StreamySpace does not run on a Mac.

- **The Microsoft Store** on PC, to install the Beta.
- **For YouTube moderation only:** I need to add your Google account email on my side first. That is why the sign-up form asks for it. Chat and viewing on YouTube will however work without it.
- **Two links:** the [Microsoft Store page](https://apps.microsoft.com/store/detail/9MZC3XQ3P7HW?cid=DevShareMCLPCS) and the [sign-up form](https://tally.so/r/BEpqM7). Please fill in the sign-up form before you install.

The Beta keeps working until 16 January 2027, which is when this test ends.

## Install and first launch

Installing takes about two minutes and works like any Microsoft Store app.

1. On your Windows PC, open the [Microsoft Store page](https://apps.microsoft.com/store/detail/9MZC3XQ3P7HW?cid=DevShareMCLPCS).
2. Press **Get** (or **Install**) and wait for it to finish.
3. Open **StreamySpace Beta** from the Start menu.
4. The first screen is **Platform connections**, step 1 of 2. That is where you sign in to relevant platforms.

If the app says the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime is missing, install it from Microsoft's site and open the app again.

## Sign in to your platforms

Sign in to each platform you use, and skip the ones you do not.

1. On **Platform connections**, press **Sign in** next to a platform. A sign-in window for that site opens.
2. Sign in the way you normally do. When it works, the status turns to "Signed in".
3. Repeat for each platform you moderate on or would like to use.
4. Press **Continue to workspace** when you are done. You can come back to this screen at any time.

Signing in lets you watch and chat. It does not prove you are a moderator, so StreamySpace cannot check your channel permissions: those still come from the platform.

### Optional: connect your accounts (Twitch, Kick, YouTube)

Press **Connect accounts** to use each platform's official moderation tools from inside StreamySpace: delete a message, time out, ban or unban from the feed. What you can do depends on your real permissions in that channel, and the app tells you when something is not allowed. You can disconnect at any time.

- **Google shows an "unverified app" screen.** That is expected during the test. Choose **Advanced**, then **Go to StreamySpace**.
- **YouTube stops working about every 7 days** during the test. Connect again when it asks.

## Set up your first workspace

A workspace is the set of streams and chats you want on screen together, and you can make as many as you like.

1. On the **Customize workspace** screen (step 2 of 2), press **Add livestream**, choose the platform, and paste the channel's URL or name.
2. Press **Add chat** for each chat you want, and paste the channel or stream URL for it.
3. Pick a layout style: **Balanced**, **Chat focus** or **Stream focus**. StreamySpace arranges the panes to fit your window.
4. Press **Open moderation view**.

In the moderation view:

- **Rearrange panes:** drag a pane's title onto another pane. The middle swaps them, and the edges place it beside, above or below.
- **Toolbar:** **Save layout**, **Reapply layout** (back to the automatic arrangement), **Reload all**, **Full screen**, **Copy diagnostics** and **Edit workspace** (back to this screen).
- **Unified Chat:** press **Configure Unified Chat** on the workspace screen to combine two or more chats into one feed.

## Things to try

You don't need to do all of these. Do what fits how you normally moderate, and tell me what felt confusing or broken.

- Add a livestream and a chat on each platform you use, then open the moderation view
- Make a workspace with four livestreams and four chats, and check that it stays usable on your screen
- Resize the window and try **Full screen**. Do the panes rearrange sensibly?
- Drag a pane's title onto another pane to swap or move it. Is the drop highlight clear?
- Press **Save layout**, close the app, reopen it and check that everything comes back
- Create a second workspace, then rename, duplicate, export and import one
- Set up **Unified Chat** with two or more chats and read the combined feed
- Try a moderation action (timeout, ban or delete) from Unified Chat, on a channel where you really are a moderator. You can use your own channel if you'd like for testing purposes
- Try the **Auto-Monitor** rules and see whether the highlights are useful or noisy
- Open the Chat Identity menu (the button next to the message box) and try a few themes
- Add a TikTok For You pane to a livestream slot
- Use StreamySpace for a real stretch of moderating (30 minutes or more) and note anything that annoys you
- Leave it running for a while and see whether it slows down or a pane stops updating

## Free vs Premium

StreamySpace will have a free plan and a paid Premium plan. In this beta you can switch between them yourself, using the plan switch on the **Platform connections** screen, so please try both.

| Feature | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Livestream panes | Up to 2 | Up to 4 |
| Chat panes | Up to 2 | Up to 4 |
| Unified Chat (one combined feed, moderation actions, Chat Identity themes) | No | Yes |
| Auto-Monitor | No | Yes |
| Connected accounts (Twitch, Kick, YouTube) | No | Yes |
| TikTok: hide follow, share and gift activity | No | Yes |
| TikTok For You pane | No | Yes |

Two things to know when you switch plans:

- Switching to **Free** trims your saved workspaces to fit: the first 2 livestreams and first 2 chats are kept, and the rest are removed.
- Switching back to **Premium** does **not** bring those panes back. If you want to keep a big workspace, export it first.

I'd like to hear which Premium features you'd actually pay for, and whether the Free plan feels usable on its own.

## Good to know

- **Moderation actions are real.** Timeouts, bans and deleted messages in Unified Chat happen on the live channel. Only test them where you're allowed to and feel comfortable.
- **Sound:** livestream panes play audio and chat panes are always muted. The TikTok For You pane stays silent until you click it.
- **Platform pages can change.** StreamySpace works by adjusting the real Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Kick pages, so a site update can break something. If a pane shows a "page changed" notice, that's useful to hear about. The app also checks for fixes automatically.
- **Chat Identity themes** style your own messages.
- **Animations:** the theme animations only play if Windows animation effects are on (Settings > Accessibility > Visual effects > Animation effects).
- **The beta expires on 16 January 2027.** After that, install the public version when it's available.

## Reporting problems

- What actually happened (a screenshot helps)
- Which platform and which plan (Free or Premium)

## Your privacy

- **Stored on your PC:** your workspaces and settings, and a separate sign-in for each platform (the same kind of login a browser keeps). Nothing is uploaded with them.
- **Sent to StreamySpace's online service:** only a small live-status check, and the sign-in step for connected accounts. Chat messages are never sent to us.
- **No analytics.** StreamySpace doesn't track how you use it. What I learn comes from your feedback and the forms.
- **Forms:** the final feedback form asks for no name or email. The emails I collected for store access are used only to give you access.
